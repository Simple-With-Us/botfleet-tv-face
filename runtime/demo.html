What we took from bloub

  • Pure sample(t). Pose math does not call Date.now() inside the pose itself. Pause, resume, and tests can re-read the same time.
  • Ease-out quint morphs. Enter/return blend pose fields. The body never overshoots.
  • Per-state morph duration. Wink and alert snap (~0.30s). Sleep eases longer (~0.55s).
  • Freeze mid-fade. Chaining states freezes the composite frame and blends from it, so there is no jump.
  • Rest = gaze + blink. Deterministic blink schedule and loop-noise gaze. No big floating bob at rest.
  • Speaking is pure too. Lip open is a multi-harmonic envelope of absolute time while talking.
